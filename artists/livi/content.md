# Tablet


### Livi
Livi is a creative technologist and installation artist from North Wales. Her practice uses satire, interactivity and speculative fiction to challenge perspectives and create mirror realities as cultural parody to explore our changing relationship with the internet. She blurs the lines between contemporary art, participatory installation, and public service announcement, to understand the societal impact of the Information Age.

### About
Tablet is a playful interactive guided meditation exploring the importance of personal and online archives. Users are prompted to press buttons, match puzzles and hold the hand of a specialised “training tool” built to guide modern web users through confronting anxieties stemming from the centralised web and reframing what the internet can be with community sharing at the forefront, with practical tools and tasks to take away at the end.

Tested at the Studio/Lab Fact Winter Social in December 2025, Audiences shared that they found the piece emotive and thought provoking. 

“This piece has shown me to to treasure and love what I have captured and created”

“I liked being prompted to meditate on what the internet could be in the future. What would a liberatory internet look like, and can we have it?”
