04. Chapter 0

How does AI influence you?

CAM_04 // CHAPTER 0 ARCHIVE // 04

Chapter 0 is a video art piece and interactive installation that follows an impressionable hacker as they drift into AI-induced psychosis, featuring a clingy chatbot that is as sickly sweet as the sticky glaze it serves.

This was an exercise in rapid-prototyping and nailing an immersive production process. It was shown and produced as artist in residence at the Immersive Experience Network Summit.
