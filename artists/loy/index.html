ARTIST: LOY
LOY

∴ surveillance · ritual technology · world-building ∴

"The following documents personal research and development experimenting with different artforms in response to the Immersive Arts UK Experiment fund as part of Accept The Cookies"

  • 01

    Gatekeeper

    A CCTV video installation that makes invisible data tracking visible, asking what it really means to click 'accept'.

  • 02

    Going Underground

    An esoteric exploration of low-tech and no-tech — drawing hand-activated portals in autonomous spaces and asking whether spirit can inhabit a machine.

  • 03

    Get Over Your Cringe

    A deck of 52 questions used to spark over 200 real conversations across the UK about what people want from a better internet.

  • 04

    Chapter 0

    A video installation following an impressionable hacker spiralling into AI-induced psychosis, shown at the Immersive Experience Network Summit.

  • 05

    Chapter -88

    A moving image piece following a glassy-eyed androgyne drifting into visions of an alternative world behind an empty fish tank, first shown at arebyte Digital Art Centre.

  • 06

    Seraphim

    A collaborative piece about biblically accurate angels in the BTS of the internet, exploring wormholing instead of doomscrolling.
