Accept the Cookies is an Immersive Arts funded arts collective using speculative fiction, gamification and interactivity to explore societies' ever changing relationship to cyberspace and the self, through a folkloric sci-fi lens. The group, spanning multiple disciplines is made up of Jim Osman, Livi Wilmore, Loy and Symoné.
